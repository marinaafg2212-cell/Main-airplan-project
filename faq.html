FAQ

Booking Questions

How can I search for and book a flight to my preferred destination?

You can search for a flight by entering your departure city, destination, travel date, and the number of passengers. After searching, you can compare available flights, review the departure and arrival times, check the airline and ticket conditions, choose the flight that best suits your needs, and complete the booking process online.

Can I choose my preferred seat before completing my flight booking?

Yes, seat selection may be available depending on the airline, flight, and type of ticket you choose. During the booking process, you can check the available seating options and select a suitable seat when the airline provides this feature. Some seats may have additional conditions or charges, so always review the information shown before confirming your selection.

Can I change my flight date, passenger information, or other booking details after making a reservation?

In many cases, changes can be made after a booking has been completed, but the available options depend on the airline and the conditions of your ticket. You may be able to change the travel date, flight time, or some passenger information. Before making any changes, check the ticket rules carefully because change fees, fare differences, or other restrictions may apply.

How can I cancel my flight booking and what should I know before requesting a cancellation?

You can check your booking details and review the cancellation conditions provided for your ticket. Depending on the airline and ticket type, you may be able to cancel your reservation online. Some tickets may be refundable while others may have cancellation fees or may not be eligible for a refund. Always review the cancellation policy before confirming your request.

What payment methods can I use when completing my flight booking online?

Available payment methods can depend on the booking service, airline, destination, and your location. During checkout, the available payment options will be displayed before you confirm your booking. Make sure that your payment information is entered correctly and that you review the total booking price, including any applicable taxes, fees, or additional services, before making the final payment.

How can I find out how much checked baggage and cabin baggage is included with my ticket?

Baggage allowances are determined by the airline, route, and type of ticket you purchase. Your booking information should show the baggage allowance associated with your flight. Before travelling, check the permitted weight and size for both cabin and checked baggage because additional baggage may require an extra payment and airlines can have different baggage policies.

What should I do if I need to change my travel date after I have already confirmed my booking?

First, open your booking details and check whether your ticket allows changes to the travel date. If changes are permitted, you can review the available alternative flights and select a new date. The airline may require you to pay a change fee or the difference between the original fare and the new fare, depending on the ticket conditions.

How can I check the current status of my flight and find out whether it is delayed or cancelled?

You can use the flight tracking feature by entering the required flight information, such as the flight number or route. The system can then display available information about the flight, including its current status, scheduled departure and arrival times, and possible delays. Always check the latest information before going to the airport because flight schedules can change.

What travel documents should I prepare before travelling on an international flight?

For international travel, passengers should check the document requirements for both their departure and destination countries. Depending on the journey, you may need a valid passport, visa, travel authorization, or other required documents. It is important to check these requirements before travelling because entry rules can vary depending on the destination and the traveller's circumstances.

How early should I arrive at the airport before my scheduled flight departure time?

The recommended arrival time can depend on the airport, airline, destination, and whether your flight is domestic or international. Arriving early gives you enough time to complete check-in, baggage drop-off, security checks, and other airport procedures without unnecessary stress. Always check your airline's instructions for the recommended arrival time for your specific flight.

What should I do if I have completed my booking but have not received my confirmation or ticket information?

First, check the email address you provided during the booking process and look in your spam or junk folder. You can also review your booking account if the reservation is connected to your profile. If you still cannot find the confirmation, check whether the payment was successfully completed and contact the appropriate booking or airline support service with your booking information for further assistance.

What should I do if I have a problem with my flight booking and need help from customer support?

If you experience a problem with your booking, first review your reservation details and identify the issue you are experiencing. You can then contact the appropriate customer support service and provide your booking reference, flight information, and a clear description of the problem. Providing accurate information can help the support team understand your request and provide the appropriate assistance as quickly as possible.
